🎃 Happy Halloween from all of us at Mimro! 🍂

Tonight is for glowing pumpkins, cozy candles and treats that don't leave a trail of plastic behind. 👻🌿

A few easy ways to have a spooky-but-sustainable night:
🕯️ Light a natural soy candle instead of a paraffin one
🎁 Wrap homemade treats in paper or beeswax wraps
👜 Trick-or-treat with a reusable tote, not a plastic bucket
🎃 Compost your jack-o'-lantern once the night is over

However you celebrate, we hope your night is full of treats and very few tricks. 🧡
