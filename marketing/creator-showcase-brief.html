Mimro Creator Showcase Brief

Creator Showcase · 1 Reel · $100 CAD Mimro Gift Card

This brief applies to the Mimro Creator Showcase only. It does not apply to the Eco Partner Program or any other creator program. It goes with the Mimro Creator Terms & Content Requirements, which remain the rules of the program.

Your brief

Create one original Reel as a screen recording of the Mimro marketplace on mimro.com, featuring Mimro Originals:
www.mimro.com/collections/mimro-originals

In your Reel:

  • Share your honest experience shopping on the Mimro marketplace.
  • Show viewers how easy it is to shop Mimro Originals, from finding a product to checking out.
  • Mention the wholesale opportunity: retailers can buy Mimro Originals at wholesale prices. [wholesale page or contact link: to be added]

Please don't state wholesale prices, discounts or minimum orders in your Reel unless Mimro has given you approved details.

What you'll receive

Once your published Reel is approved, you'll receive a $100 CAD Mimro Gift Card by email, to use on Mimro products. No creator fee or other cash payment is included. The gift card is non-refundable, non-transferable and can't be redeemed for cash. Shipping costs on gift card purchases may apply at checkout.

How it works

  1. Review this brief and the Mimro Creator Terms & Content Requirements.
  2. Create your clips and edit them into one Reel.
  3. Send your Reel in for publishing approval within 10 days of selection. Please don't publish yet. Mimro will generally send feedback within 5 business days.
  4. Make any revisions requested (up to two reasonable rounds, generally within 3 to 5 business days).
  5. Publish within 3 days of final approval, with a clear disclosure (#ad or a Paid Partnership label).
  6. Send Mimro the link to your published Reel. Once Mimro confirms it, your gift card is emailed.

Video requirements

  • Vertical 9:16 format, at least 1080 × 1920, clear audio and good lighting.
  • Properly licensed music and third-party materials.
  • Original, unedited video files on request.
  • Be honest and accurate: no unsupported environmental, health or performance claims.

Need help?

Reply to your selection email. Your Creator Representative can answer questions and give feedback before you submit your draft. Please tell Mimro as soon as possible if you may miss a deadline.
