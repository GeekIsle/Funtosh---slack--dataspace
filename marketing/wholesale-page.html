Wholesale FAQ

Is there a minimum order?

No. Every SKU ships 1 unit per case, so you can try the whole range in small quantities.

When will I get my invoice?

Usually within 1 business day of your PO. It comes from Mimro by email with a secure link to review and pay.

How do I pay a direct order?

Open the invoice email, check the items, shipping and taxes, then pay online from the link. We ship once payment is received.

Can I get payment terms?

Yes, order through Faire for Net 60 terms. Faire handles the payment terms; we fulfil your order.

Where do you ship from?

Our warehouse in Brampton, Ontario. Shipping cost is shown on your invoice before you pay.

Can I change my order after sending the PO?

Yes. Reply to your invoice email or contact support@mimro.com before paying and we'll update it.
