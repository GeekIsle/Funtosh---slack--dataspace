The Mimro story

Everyday essentials

Every day, sponges, paper towels and plastic scrubbers end up in the trash. At Mimro, we knew the things we reach for most could be made better.

So we made our own. Mimro Originals are plant-based kitchen and bath essentials made from materials like coconut fibre, cellulose, cotton and natural loofah — designed to work hard every day and leave less behind.

Alongside our Originals, Mimro curates low-waste swaps from trusted brands, so building a lower-plastic home is simple.
