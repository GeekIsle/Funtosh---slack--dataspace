Convenience is supposed to make life easier, and in the moment, it does. A disposable wipe for a sticky counter. A plastic-wrapped snack because you’re late. A quick replacement because you ran out and don’t have time to compare.
None of it feels like a big deal. But convenience has a hidden cost, and not only to the planet. It creates a loop of restocking, throwing away, and spending a little more than you planned. The trash fills quietly. Cabinets fill with half-used products. And the busier life gets, the more those shortcuts become your default.
Most people don’t choose waste because they don’t care. They choose it because they’re tired, busy, and the “better option” feels like extra steps. Mimro exists to remove that friction.
## The habit: Pause and Place
You don’t need a list of 50 swaps. You need one habit that interrupts the convenience loop. For the next 7 days, every time you reach for a disposable shortcut:
1. **Pause** and name what you’re actually solving. A mess? A time crunch? A comfort need? A “just in case” worry?
2. **Place** one reusable or refill-friendly option exactly where you naturally reach. Not in a drawer you’ll forget. Right where the shortcut usually lives.
Why it works: convenience wins because it’s closer. When the better option is the closest option, you don’t need motivation. You just need placement.
Start with one high-repeat moment:
- Wiping counters and quick spills
- Removing makeup or refreshing your face
- Period care and bathroom restocks
- On-the-go moments (tissues, wipes, sanitizer)
- Laundry and home-care refills
