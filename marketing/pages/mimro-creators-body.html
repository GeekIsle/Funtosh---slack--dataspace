Mimro Creator Terms & Content Requirements

Compensation

Selected creators receive a $100 CAD Mimro Gift Card and a $100 CAD Creator Fee upon successful completion of all campaign requirements. Shipping costs may apply at checkout and are the creator's responsibility unless Mimro states otherwise in writing. The Gift Card may only be used on Mimro, is non-refundable, non-transferable and can't be redeemed for cash. The Creator Fee is paid within 14 days after all deliverables are submitted, approved and published, and Mimro has received a valid invoice.

Deliverables & approval

One Reel, one Feed Post and one Story, plus any campaign-specific requirements. All content must be submitted to Mimro for approval before publication. Mimro generally replies within 5 business days and may request up to 2 reasonable rounds of revisions for factual accuracy, missing requirements, unsupported claims, disclosure, brand safety or technical issues. Mimro will not ask you to change a genuine opinion because it is unfavorable.

Timeline

Order products within 7 days of receiving the Gift Card. Tell Mimro within 24 hours of delivery. Submit your first draft within 10 days of delivery. Complete revisions within 3–5 business days. Publish within 3 days of final approval. Contact Mimro as soon as possible if you may miss a deadline.

Environmental & product claims

Base claims on information from Mimro, the product page or approved campaign materials. Avoid absolute claims such as "100% sustainable", "completely eco-friendly" or "zero environmental impact" unless substantiated and approved. Present personal experiences as personal experiences.

Disclosure, music & originality

Clearly disclose your relationship with Mimro (e.g. #ad, #sponsored, #gifted, Paid Partnership) as required by the Competition Bureau Canada, the U.S. FTC where applicable, and platform rules. You must have rights to all music, footage, images and fonts used, and permission from anyone identifiable in your content. Disclose any AI-generated or synthetic material to Mimro before submission.

Content license

You keep ownership of your content. On approval, you grant Mimro a non-exclusive, worldwide, 12-month license to use it on Mimro-owned channels including social media, mimro.com, product pages, email and digital ads. Mimro may resize, crop, caption or reformat it without materially altering your likeness or opinion. After 12 months, Mimro won't start new uses without renewed permission, and will make reasonable efforts to remove content on request.

Incomplete campaigns & relationship

If required deliverables aren't completed after reasonable opportunity, Mimro may end the collaboration and the Creator Fee isn't payable; a Gift Card already issued or used isn't refundable or redeemable for cash. Creators participate as independent creators, not employees, agents or partners, and are responsible for their own expenses and taxes. Mimro may request performance info (URLs, views, engagement) about 7 days after posting. Participation doesn't guarantee future campaigns.
