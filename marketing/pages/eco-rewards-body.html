Mimro Eco Points terms

Free to play, no purchase necessary. One claim per person (email) per weekly Eco-Quest. Each Eco-Quest closes Sunday at 11:59 p.m. ET and a new one starts Monday. Points stay in your balance until you choose to redeem them. You can redeem from your points card in multiples of 500 points, with every 500 points worth $5 CAD off. When you redeem, your discount code appears on your points card (usually within the hour) and the redeemed points are deducted from your balance; each code is single-use and valid on mimro.com for 12 months. Points and codes have no cash value, can't be transferred and can't be combined with other discount codes (free shipping on orders over $75 still applies). Earn 2 points for every $1 spent on eligible purchases, excluding taxes and shipping. Purchase points are added to your balance after the order is paid, and points from refunded or cancelled orders are removed. Eligible purchases from October 12, 2025 to October 11, 2026 were credited once on October 12, 2026, including a one-time +100 welcome bonus. Mimro may remove points from claims that look automated or duplicated.
