Mimro Eco Partner Onboarding Guide

Eco Partner Program · Create. Share. Inspire. Earn.

Welcome to the Mimro Eco Partner Program! This guide shows you how to set up your account, get your affiliate links, choose products to promote and request samples. You'll find everything in your Eco Partner dashboard at www.partners.mimro.com.

As an Eco Partner you earn 15% commission on eligible referral sales, and customers you refer get 10% off eligible purchases with your link or code.

1Update your payment information

You can update your payment method and payment details directly through your account.

If you are unable to update your payment information, please contact your Mimro Creator Representative at marketing@mimro.com for assistance.

2Your default affiliate link

Every creator is assigned a unique affiliate link to track traffic and referrals. Here is an example of what a default affiliate link looks like (yours will be different):

https://www.mimro.com?sca_ref=11875335.hP0S9zk7W1kDqn

Use this link to direct your audience to Mimro's website. Any eligible referrals made through your link are tracked automatically.

3Customize your affiliate link

You can also create a personalized affiliate link that is easier to share and remember. For example:

https://www.mimro.com/sam

Your custom link can be used in your social media bios, videos, posts, and other promotional content.

4Select products to promote

Browse the available Mimro product catalogue and select the products you are interested in promoting.

  • Click on a product to generate or access its affiliate link.
  • Select the Mimro products that best fit your content and audience.
  • Copy the individual affiliate link for each product you want to promote.

5Share your affiliate links

You have two ways to direct your audience to Mimro:

Option A: Individual product links. Share a direct affiliate link to a specific product. This is ideal when featuring a product in a video, post, review, or recommendation.

Option B: Your customized affiliate link. Share your personalized Mimro link to direct your audience to the store, where they can browse and shop for products.

Tip: Use individual product links when promoting a specific item, and your customized affiliate link when introducing your audience to Mimro as a whole.

6Request product samples

Want to try a product before promoting it? You can request a sample from Mimro Originals to experience it firsthand before creating promotional content.

Submit your sample request here: www.mimro.com/pages/request-a-sample

  • Pick the Mimro Originals product you'd like to feature and enter your shipping details.
  • A $4.99 shipping fee applies when a sample is shipped ($4.99 CAD to Canada, $4.99 USD to the United States). It is not a fee to join the program.
  • Once submitted, your request will be reviewed by the Mimro team. Samples are provided at Mimro's discretion, depend on availability, and are not guaranteed.

Good to remember

  • Disclose your partnership clearly (#ad, #affiliate, #gifted or a Paid Partnership label) whenever required.
  • Share honest experiences and don't make environmental or health claims you can't back up.
  • Use your own link or code so your referrals are tracked.
  • Read the full Eco Partner Program Terms & Requirements for the rules on content, commission and samples.

Need help?

Contact your Mimro Creator Representative at marketing@mimro.com. We'll guide you through onboarding and the creative process.
