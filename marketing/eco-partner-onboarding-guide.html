Mimro Eco Partner Onboarding Guide

Mimro Eco Partner Program for Creators · Create. Share. Inspire. Earn.

Welcome to the Mimro Eco Partner Program for Creators! This guide walks you through your Eco Partner dashboard, step by step, and matches the Mimro Eco-Partner video tutorial (Steps 1 to 7). You'll find everything in your dashboard at partners.mimro.com.

As an Eco Partner you earn 15% commission on eligible referral sales, and customers you refer get 10% off eligible purchases with your link or code.

Before you start: update your payment information

You can update your payment method and payment details directly through your account. If you are unable to update your payment information, please contact your Mimro Creator Representative at marketing@mimro.com for assistance.

1Explore your dashboard

Find your affiliate links and a performance overview on your dashboard, including your pending, approved and paid commission.

Every creator is assigned a unique default affiliate link to track traffic and referrals. Here is an example of what it looks like (yours will be different):

https://www.mimro.com?sca_ref=11875335.hP0S9zk7W1kDqn

Use it to direct your audience to Mimro's website. Any eligible referrals made through your link are tracked automatically.

2Customize your affiliate link

Open the editor next to your custom affiliate link, enter a custom ending, and save. This gives you a personalized link that is easier to share and remember. For example:

https://www.mimro.com/sam

Use your custom link in your social media bios, videos, posts, and other promotional content.

3Open the product link tool

Click Get product link on your dashboard. A window opens where you can create an affiliate link for individual Mimro products.

4Find a product to promote

Search for a product and select it from the results. Browse the Mimro product catalogue and choose the products that best fit your content and your audience.

5Generate product affiliate links

Click Generate to create the affiliate link for the product you selected. Then repeat for each product you want to feature. Each product gets its own link, and your saved links appear in the list below.

6Find and share your saved links

Your saved links are listed in the same window. Use the copy control to copy a product link and share it with your audience.

You have two ways to direct your audience to Mimro:

Option A: Individual product links. Share a direct affiliate link to a specific product. This is ideal when featuring a product in a video, post, review, or recommendation.

Option B: Your customized affiliate link. Share your personalized Mimro link to direct your audience to the store, where they can browse and shop for products.

Tip: Use individual product links when promoting a specific item, and your customized affiliate link when introducing your audience to Mimro as a whole.

7Return to your dashboard

Close the product link window to return to your dashboard. You can come back and generate more links at any time.

Bonus: request product samples

Want to try a product before promoting it? You can request a sample from Mimro Originals to experience it firsthand before creating promotional content.

Submit your sample request here: www.mimro.com/pages/request-a-sample

  • Pick the Mimro Originals product you'd like to feature and enter your shipping details.
  • A $4.99 shipping fee is paid at checkout to process your order.
  • Your request is reviewed by the Mimro team. Samples are provided at Mimro's discretion, depend on availability, and are not guaranteed.

Good to remember

  • Disclose your partnership clearly (#ad, #affiliate, #gifted or a Paid Partnership label) whenever required.
  • Share honest experiences and don't make environmental or health claims you can't back up.
  • Use your own link or code so your referrals are tracked.
  • Read the full Eco Partner Program Terms & Requirements for the rules on content, commission and samples.

Need help?

Contact your Mimro Creator Representative at marketing@mimro.com. We'll guide you through onboarding and the creative process.
