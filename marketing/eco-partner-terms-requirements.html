Mimro Eco Partner Program Terms & Requirements

Eco Partner Program · 15% commission · 10% off for referred customers · Version of September 29, 2026

The Mimro Eco Partner Program is an ongoing creator and affiliate partnership for individuals who want to create authentic content around Mimro, promote more sustainable everyday choices, and earn from the sales they generate.

Eco Partners may create and share content featuring the Mimro brand, Mimro Originals, and products available on the Mimro website through social media, websites, blogs, videos, communities, and other approved digital channels.

Eco Partners earn a 15% commission on eligible referral sales generated through their unique affiliate link or coupon code. Referred customers receive a 10% discount when they make an eligible purchase using the Eco Partner's unique affiliate link or coupon code.

There is no fee to enroll in the program. Selected Eco Partners may receive complimentary samples from Mimro Originals for content creation. A $4.99 shipping charge applies when a complimentary sample is shipped. Samples are not guaranteed.

The Mimro Eco Partner Program does not include a fixed Creator Fee or Mimro Gift Card.

Compensation

Eco Partners earn a 15% commission on eligible referral sales when a customer completes a qualifying purchase through the Eco Partner's unique affiliate link or uses their assigned coupon code.

Eco Partners may receive:

  • 15% commission on eligible referral sales
  • A unique Mimro affiliate link
  • A unique coupon code that gives referred customers 10% off eligible purchases, where applicable
  • Access to approved Mimro promotional and brand materials
  • Opportunities to participate in selected Mimro campaigns and product launches
  • Eligibility to receive complimentary samples from Mimro Originals

There is no fixed Creator Fee for participating in the program. Participation does not guarantee any minimum level of sales, commissions, products, or earnings.

Enrollment and Sample Shipping

Enrollment in the Mimro Eco Partner Program is free. There is no enrollment fee.

Selected Eco Partners who accept a complimentary Mimro Originals sample pay $4.99 for shipping when the sample is shipped. The shipping charge is not a fee to join the program. Enrollment does not guarantee referral sales, commissions, complimentary samples, paid campaigns, Mimro Gift Cards, Creator Fees, or any minimum level of earnings.

Referred Customer Discount

Customers referred by an Eco Partner receive a 10% discount on eligible purchases when they use the Eco Partner's unique affiliate link or coupon code at checkout. The discount:

  • Applies only to eligible products and qualifying orders
  • Is subject to Mimro's current promotion and discount rules
  • May not be combined with other discounts or promotions unless Mimro permits it
  • Has no cash value

The customer discount is separate from the Eco Partner's 15% commission.

Eligible Referral Sales

Commission is earned only on eligible, completed referral sales successfully attributed to the Eco Partner through Mimro's affiliate tracking system. Orders may be excluded from commission eligibility if they are:

  • Cancelled
  • Refunded
  • Returned
  • Fraudulent
  • Duplicated
  • Charged back
  • Otherwise determined to be ineligible under Mimro's program rules

If commission has already been credited for an order that is subsequently refunded, returned, cancelled, or charged back, the corresponding commission may be deducted from the Eco Partner's account or future commission balance. Commission attribution, approval, and payment are based on Mimro's affiliate platform records.

Affiliate Links & Coupon Codes

Each approved Eco Partner may receive a unique affiliate link and/or coupon code for sharing with their audience. Eco Partners are encouraged to use these tracking methods whenever promoting Mimro or eligible products.

Eco Partners must not:

  • Manipulate Mimro's affiliate tracking system
  • Generate fraudulent or artificial transactions
  • Misrepresent coupon values, discounts, promotions, or expiration dates
  • Create misleading advertisements that appear to be official Mimro advertisements
  • Impersonate Mimro through websites, social media accounts, advertisements, or other channels
  • Publish affiliate links or coupon codes through unauthorized spam
  • Place coupon codes on unauthorized coupon or deal aggregation websites without Mimro's permission
  • Use Mimro trademarks or brand terms in paid-search advertising without prior written authorization
  • Use their affiliate relationship in a deceptive or misleading manner

Mimro reserves the right to investigate suspicious transactions and withhold or reverse commissions associated with fraudulent or otherwise ineligible activity.

Complimentary Product Samples

Selected Eco Partners may receive complimentary samples from Mimro Originals to support authentic product experiences and content creation. Eco Partners become eligible for sample consideration after completing free enrollment. If selected to receive a complimentary sample, the Eco Partner pays a $4.99 shipping charge for the sample shipment. Completing enrollment does not guarantee that an Eco Partner will receive a sample.

Complimentary samples:

  • Are provided at Mimro's discretion
  • Are limited to selected products from Mimro Originals
  • Are subject to product availability
  • May depend on partner eligibility, content quality, audience relevance, performance, or campaign requirements
  • Are intended to support authentic content creation
  • Are not guaranteed as part of Eco Partner membership
  • Are not redeemable or exchangeable for cash

Products from third-party brands available on Mimro are not included in the complimentary sample program unless specifically authorized by Mimro for a particular campaign or collaboration. Receiving a complimentary sample does not guarantee additional samples, paid collaborations, or participation in future campaigns.

Content Requirements

Eco Partners are expected to create authentic and original content that introduces their audience to Mimro and encourages meaningful engagement with the brand. Content may be created around:

  • The Mimro brand
  • Products available on Mimro
  • Mimro Originals
  • Sustainable living and low-waste habits
  • Everyday sustainable choices
  • Product routines and practical uses
  • Lifestyle topics relevant to Mimro's mission

Content may include Reels, TikTok videos, YouTube Shorts, product demonstrations, product reviews, unboxing videos, lifestyle content, social media posts, Stories, tutorials, sustainable living tips, blog or website content, and other approved creative formats.

Eco Partners may create content around third-party products available for purchase through Mimro. However, complimentary samples provided through the program are limited to Mimro Originals, unless otherwise specifically authorized by Mimro.

Content Guidelines

Do

Eco Partners should:

  • Create original, authentic, creator-led content
  • Show products being used naturally in everyday life
  • Share honest experiences and opinions
  • Clearly identify Mimro when directing audiences to Mimro products
  • Focus on practical product use and realistic lifestyle benefits
  • Highlight achievable sustainable habits and routines
  • Present products in clean and visually appealing environments
  • Use natural lighting and clear audio whenever possible
  • Accurately represent product features and functionality
  • Use Mimro-provided product information when discussing specific product attributes
  • Include their affiliate link or coupon code when appropriate
  • Properly disclose their relationship with Mimro

Don't

Eco Partners must not:

  • Make environmental or sustainability claims that cannot be substantiated
  • Make unauthorized health, medical, therapeutic, or scientific claims
  • Misrepresent product functionality, materials, certifications, ingredients, performance, or benefits
  • Make false or deceptive statements
  • Directly disparage competing brands or creators
  • Imply that a third-party brand sponsors or endorses the Eco Partner unless authorized
  • Use excessive editing that materially misrepresents a product
  • Publish offensive, discriminatory, unlawful, or harmful content
  • Misrepresent complimentary products as independently purchased when disclosure is required
  • Engage in fraudulent or artificial methods of generating traffic, referrals, orders, or commissions

Technical Content Recommendations

For short-form video content, Eco Partners are encouraged to produce content using a vertical format (9:16), a minimum resolution of 1080 × 1920, clear high-quality audio, good lighting, clear product visibility, clean and appropriate backgrounds, and platform-appropriate video length. Content should be suitable for platforms such as Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Facebook, and other relevant digital channels.

Unless Mimro provides campaign-specific requirements, Eco Partners retain reasonable creative freedom over how they present their authentic experiences with Mimro products.

Disclosure Requirements

Eco Partners must clearly disclose their relationship with Mimro whenever required by applicable advertising laws, regulations, and platform policies. Appropriate disclosures may include #ad, #sponsored, #affiliate, #gifted, Paid Partnership labels, or other equivalent disclosures required by the platform or jurisdiction.

When an Eco Partner may earn commission from a link or coupon code, the affiliate relationship must be clearly disclosed where required. When an Eco Partner receives a complimentary product, the gifted relationship must also be appropriately disclosed.

Eco Partners are responsible for complying with applicable advertising and endorsement requirements, including Competition Bureau Canada, FTC, and platform-specific requirements. Failure to properly disclose the relationship may result in a request to correct the content, suspension of program benefits, or termination from the program.

Environmental & Product Claims

Eco Partners must not make environmental, scientific, health, performance, certification, or sustainability claims that cannot be reasonably substantiated. When discussing specific environmental attributes, Eco Partners should rely on product information provided by Mimro or on the applicable Mimro product page.

Eco Partners should avoid absolute or broad claims such as "100% sustainable," "zero environmental impact," or similar statements unless Mimro has specifically provided substantiation. Personal opinions and experiences should be clearly presented as such.

Music & Copyright Compliance

Music, photographs, graphics, footage, fonts, or other third-party materials included in Eco Partner content must be appropriately licensed or otherwise authorized for the intended use. Music should be properly licensed by the Eco Partner or sourced from a platform library that permits the intended commercial or promotional use.

Mimro may request that content associated with the program be corrected or removed if it contains unauthorized copyrighted material.

Content Ownership & Mimro Use of Content

The Eco Partner retains ownership of original content they create. The Eco Partner represents that they are the creator or authorized owner of their submitted content, that they have the authority to publish it, that they have obtained any necessary permissions from individuals appearing in it, and that it does not knowingly infringe another person's rights.

Mimro may interact with, link to, or share publicly available Eco Partner content through functionality provided by the applicable platform. Any broader commercial use by Mimro, including paid advertising, product pages, email campaigns, or landing pages, may require additional permission or separate campaign-specific usage terms. Participation in the program alone does not grant Mimro an unrestricted perpetual license to all content created by the Eco Partner.

Brand Safety & Honest Reviews

Eco Partners should represent Mimro professionally. Content associated with the program must not knowingly contain discriminatory or hateful material, illegal activity, fraudulent or deceptive information, explicit sexual content, threatening or violent material, deliberately false product information, or content intended to materially damage or misrepresent Mimro.

Any use of AI-generated imagery, voices, avatars, product representations, or other synthetic content in a Mimro-specific campaign should be disclosed to Mimro where it could reasonably be mistaken for authentic product footage or a real testimonial.

Mimro encourages Eco Partners to share genuine experiences. Eco Partners are not required to provide positive reviews or make statements they do not believe. Any factual claims about a product should remain accurate and supportable.

Performance Reporting

Mimro may evaluate Eco Partner performance using information available through its affiliate, e-commerce, and marketing systems, including affiliate link clicks, coupon code usage, referral orders, conversion rates, eligible referral sales, and commission generated. Mimro may also request relevant content performance information, such as published content URLs, reach, views, engagement, story screenshots, audience interactions, and platform insights.

Performance information may be considered when determining eligibility for complimentary samples, new Mimro Originals launches, special campaigns, featured Eco Partner opportunities, or future paid collaborations. High performance does not automatically guarantee future campaigns, products, or compensation.

Program Participation & Independent Relationship

Participation does not guarantee complimentary products, a minimum number of referrals, minimum earnings, paid campaigns, Creator Fees, Mimro Gift Cards, future collaborations, or continued participation. Eco Partners are responsible for their own equipment, content production, internet access, taxes, expenses, and other costs unless Mimro specifically agrees otherwise in writing.

Eco Partners participate as independent participants. Nothing in these Terms creates an employment, agency, franchise, joint venture, or legal partnership relationship between Mimro and an Eco Partner. Eco Partners do not have authority to enter into agreements, make commitments, make warranties, or bind Mimro to any legal or financial obligation, and must not represent themselves as Mimro employees.

Program Suspension & Termination

Mimro may suspend or terminate participation in the Eco Partner Program for reasonable program, brand safety, compliance, or fraud-prevention reasons, including:

  • Fraudulent referral activity
  • Artificial traffic or transactions
  • Misleading advertising
  • Repeated violations of Mimro's content or brand guidelines
  • Unsubstantiated product or sustainability claims
  • Failure to properly disclose affiliate or gifted relationships
  • Copyright or intellectual property violations
  • Abuse of affiliate links, coupon codes, or complimentary products
  • Impersonation of Mimro
  • Conduct that creates material legal, reputational, or customer risk for Mimro

Where appropriate, Mimro may give the Eco Partner an opportunity to correct a violation before termination. Serious fraud, unlawful conduct, deliberate deception, or material brand-safety violations may result in immediate suspension or termination. Upon termination, the Eco Partner must stop representing themselves as an active Mimro Eco Partner and discontinue unauthorized use of Mimro promotional materials. Eligible commissions earned before termination will be handled according to Mimro's applicable commission policies, subject to adjustments for refunds, returns, chargebacks, fraud, or other ineligible transactions.

Changes to the Program

Mimro may update the Eco Partner Program from time to time, including the sample shipping charge, commission structure, referred-customer discount, eligible products and orders, tracking methods, eligibility requirements, complimentary-sample criteria, benefits, content guidelines, or these Terms & Requirements. Material changes affecting existing Eco Partners will be communicated through reasonable channels, such as email, the Eco Partner dashboard, or the Mimro website. Continued participation after an updated version becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the updated program terms, subject to applicable law.
