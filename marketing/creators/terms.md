## About the Mimro Creator Program
The Mimro Creator Program (Creator Showcase) is a content collaboration for selected creators only. Creators apply, and selected creators create a video promoting Mimro products or Mimro's free weekly Eco-Quest, as set out in their campaign brief.
After their published content is approved, creators receive a **$100 CAD Mimro Gift Card** to purchase products from Mimro. There is no cash payment or Creator Fee for this program.
Creators are expected to personally use the featured products or play the Eco-Quest themselves, and create original social media content about it.
## Compensation
Creators who complete the Mimro Creator Program receive:
- **$100 CAD Mimro Gift Card** to purchase products from Mimro, issued by email only after the required content has been published and approved by Mimro.
No Creator Fee or other cash payment is included.
Shipping costs on gift card purchases may apply and will be displayed at checkout. Shipping costs are the responsibility of the creator unless Mimro states otherwise in writing.
The Mimro Gift Card:
- May only be used to purchase products from Mimro.
- Is non-refundable.
- Is non-transferable.
- Cannot be redeemed for cash.
The campaign is complete, and the gift card is issued, when all of the following conditions are met:
- All required deliverables have been submitted
- Content has been approved by Mimro
- Approved content has been published
- The creator has shared the published content links with Mimro
## Required Deliverables
Each creator is required to complete the following:
- One (1) Reel (video) promoting either Mimro products (showing them in the creator's real routine and where to find them on mimro.com) or Mimro's weekly Eco-Quest (mimro.com/pages/eco-rewards): play that week's Eco-Quest on camera, share the week's eco swap (or one from the creator's own routine), and invite viewers to play and earn Mimro Eco Points
- One (1) Story supporting the Reel, with a link sticker to the featured product page or mimro.com/pages/eco-rewards
The campaign brief states whether to promote Mimro products or Eco-Quest. Eco-Quest content must only use facts shown on the Eco-Quest page and must not promise prizes or amounts that aren't listed there.
Campaign-specific instructions may provide additional requirements.
All social media content must be submitted to Mimro for approval before publication.
## Content Requirements
Creator content should authentically show the creator's experience with the featured Mimro products or with Mimro's weekly Eco-Quest.
Product content may demonstrate how the product is used, how it fits into an everyday routine, its practical benefits, and the creator's personal experience.
Eco-Quest content may demonstrate:
- Playing that week's Eco-Quest and the creator's score and points.
- The week's eco swap, or a low-waste swap from the creator's own routine.
- How Mimro Eco Points work, using only the facts on the Eco-Quest page.
- The creator's personal experience.
- A clear call to action to play at mimro.com/pages/eco-rewards.
Content should feel natural, informative, relatable, and consistent with the creator's usual style.
## Content Guidelines
### Do
Creators should:
- Create original, creator-led content.
- Show the featured product, or the Eco-Quest page and gameplay, clearly.
- Share genuine experiences and opinions.
- Clearly identify the product and/or Mimro.
- Use accurate product information.
- Focus on practical uses and realistic lifestyle benefits.
- Use good lighting, clear audio, and a clean visual environment.
- Follow all required advertising and partnership disclosure rules.
### Don't
Creators should not:
- Make unsupported environmental or sustainability claims.
- Make unauthorized medical, therapeutic, health, or scientific claims.
- Misrepresent a product's materials, certifications, ingredients, functionality, performance, or benefits.
- Make false or deceptive statements.
- Present opinions or experiences they do not genuinely hold.
- Make misleading direct comparisons with competitors.
- Use excessive editing or effects that materially misrepresent the product.
- Include offensive, discriminatory, unlawful, or harmful content.
- Present gifted or compensated products in a misleading manner.
## Environmental & Product Claims
Creators must use responsible language when discussing environmental benefits, sustainability, product performance, or certifications.
Claims should be based on information provided by Mimro, the applicable Mimro product page, or other approved campaign materials.
Creators should avoid absolute claims such as "100% sustainable," "completely eco-friendly," or "zero environmental impact" unless the claim has been specifically substantiated and approved for use.
Personal experiences should be presented as personal experiences rather than universal product claims.
## Content Approval
All required social media content must be submitted to Mimro for review before publication.
Mimro will generally provide feedback within five (5) business days.
Creators may be asked to complete up to two (2) reasonable rounds of revisions to address:
- Factual inaccuracies.
- Missing campaign requirements.
- Unsupported claims.
- Disclosure issues.
- Brand-safety concerns.
- Technical or formatting issues.
Mimro will not require a creator to change a genuine opinion simply because it is unfavorable.
## Content Timeline
Unless otherwise specified in the campaign brief:
- The first content drafts (Reel and Story) should be submitted within ten (10) days of selection.
- Requested revisions should generally be completed within three to five (3–5) business days.
- Approved content should be published within three (3) days of final approval.
- The Mimro Gift Card is emailed after Mimro confirms the published Reel and Story.
Creators should contact Mimro as soon as reasonably possible if circumstances may prevent them from meeting a deadline.
## Technical Content Requirements
Unless otherwise specified, short-form video should:
- Use a vertical 9:16 format.
- Have a minimum recommended resolution of 1080 × 1920.
- Include clear, high-quality audio.
- Clearly show the featured product or the Eco-Quest page and gameplay.
- Use appropriate lighting.
- Be suitable for platforms such as Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, and Meta placements.
Creators should also provide the original, unedited video files used to produce the final approved Reel when requested by Mimro.
## Advertising & Partnership Disclosure
Creators must clearly disclose their relationship with Mimro whenever required by applicable advertising laws, regulations, or platform policies.
Depending on the content and platform, disclosures may include:
- #ad
- #sponsored
- #gifted
- Paid Partnership
- Other clear disclosure language appropriate to the platform
Disclosures should be clear, prominent, and easy for viewers to understand.
Creators are responsible for complying with applicable requirements, including those of the Competition Bureau Canada, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission where applicable, and individual social media platforms.

The creator retains ownership of all content created under the collaboration.
Upon approval of the content, the creator grants Mimro a non-exclusive, worldwide license for twelve (12) months to use the approved content across Mimro-owned and operated marketing channels, including:
- Mimro social media accounts.
- Mimro.com and related landing pages.
- Mimro's Shopify storefront.
- Product pages.
- Email marketing.
- Digital advertising.
- Promotional and campaign materials.
Mimro may resize, crop, edit, caption, reformat, or combine approved content with other marketing materials as reasonably necessary for these uses, provided such modifications do not materially alter the creator's likeness or misrepresent the creator or their expressed opinion.
After the twelve-month license period, previously published uses may remain in circulation or in Mimro's historical content, but Mimro will not initiate new campaigns or new promotional uses of the content without renewed permission where required.
Creators may request removal after the license period, and Mimro will make commercially reasonable efforts to remove the content from channels under its direct control.
## Brand Safety
Content created under the Mimro Creator Program must not contain:
- Hateful, discriminatory, threatening, or unlawful material.
- Explicit sexual content.
- Fraudulent or intentionally deceptive information.
- Deliberately false product information.
- Content that materially misrepresents Mimro or the featured product.
Creators should avoid prominently featuring unrelated third-party trademarks or logos in a way that could incorrectly imply sponsorship or endorsement.
If AI-generated or synthetic imagery, voices, avatars, or other material is used in campaign content, the creator should disclose this to Mimro before submission where the material could reasonably be mistaken for authentic product footage or a genuine testimonial.

Mimro may request reasonable campaign performance information, including:
- Published content URLs.
- Story screenshots.
- Views or reach.
- Engagement.
- Relevant platform insights.
Performance information may generally be requested approximately seven (7) days after publication.
Performance results may be considered when selecting creators for future Mimro campaigns, product launches, ongoing partnerships, or other creator opportunities.
Participation does not guarantee future campaigns.
## Incomplete Campaigns
If a creator does not complete the required deliverables after being given reasonable opportunities to address outstanding requirements, Mimro may terminate the collaboration.
No Mimro Gift Card is issued for an incomplete campaign.
Mimro reserves the right to restrict participation in future creator campaigns where program requirements have not been completed.
## Independent Creator Relationship
Participation in the Mimro Creator Program does not create an employment, agency, franchise, joint venture, or legal partnership relationship between the creator and Mimro.
Creators participate as independent creators and are responsible for their own equipment, content-production expenses, internet access, taxes, and other expenses unless Mimro agrees otherwise in writing.
Creators do not have authority to enter into agreements or make commitments on behalf of Mimro.
## About Mimro
Mimro is a Canadian eco-friendly marketplace focused on making more sustainable everyday choices practical and accessible.
Our selection includes plastic-free alternatives, low-waste household essentials, sustainable personal care, eco-friendly home products, reusable everyday solutions, and Mimro Originals.
Our goal is to help individuals and families discover practical products that can reduce everyday waste and support more thoughtful consumption.
